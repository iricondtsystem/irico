Through our group companies, we provide end-to-end solutions—from advanced inspection to precision casting upgradation —ensuring structural integrity and performance reliability.

IRICO has highly qualified and dynamic NDE Engineers with vast experience in the field of NDT. To back up their efforts, IRICO has dedicated professional team of trained Engineers to the requirements of AERB / Bhabha Atomic Research Center – BARC, American Society for Non-Destructive Testing – ASNT (SNT-TC-1A), Indian Society for Non-Destructive Testing – ISNT (IS 13805), British Institute of Non-Destructive Testing – BINDT (PCN), European Standard – BS EN ISO 9712 and European Pressure Equipment Directive – PED 2014/68/EU

IRICO is approved by Competent Authority RASD, AERB to operate Linear Accelerator 9 Mev (Dual Energy), Linac 6 Mev, 100 Ci Cobalt-60, 100 Ci Iridium-192 Isotope & Portable X-Ray Upto 300 kV.
